## 3. Target Customer Avatar

**Single avatar. All content and offer copy written for this person only.**

### The Invisible Performer (internal name — never use it in client-facing copy)

- **Profile:** 2–5 years into engineering. Technically solid, good reviews, trusted to deliver — not yet seen as promotion-ready.
- **Core pain:** Manager keeps saying "you're doing great," but the promotion isn't happening.
- **Pain points:** Stuck in low-leverage tasks, impact invisible outside their team, unproven with senior leaders, doesn't know how to self-advocate without sounding cringe.
- **Emotional state:** Quietly wondering if being good at the job just isn't enough. Doesn't want to become "the annoying self-promoter with a personal brand and a ring light."
- **Budget sensitivity:** Employed, moderate — will pay if it's tied to a believable promotion/income jump, not sold as generic motivation.

## 4. The Promotion-Ready Engineer Blueprint (PRIMARY — active offer)

**Canonical live copy:** `offer/index.html`.

### Promise

Over 6 months, turn from "solid engineer who gets good feedback" into someone with a clear promotion case a manager can actually advocate for. First 12 weeks build the Promotion-Ready Plan; the next 12 weeks apply it inside the real job. Not a promise that the company promotes on a specific date — the promise is the controllable part: proof, visibility, and trust.

### Format

6-month founder cohort, weekly calls. Build Sprint (first 12 weeks: kickoff + 9 core missions + closeout) → Application Sprint (next 12 weeks: applying the plan at work). Skool community access from day one.

### The 3 Program Layers (maps to the Magic Model)

1. **Next-Level Work** — master current workload, make work teachable, expand scope.
2. **Known for Impact** — demonstrate value, spread impact, reach leaders.
3. **Trusted by Seniors** — meet commitments, own outcomes, influence others.

### Pricing & Payment (Founder's Case Study Group) — **updated Aug 2026**

- **$1,000 paid in full**, or **5 payments of $250** ($1,250 total — paid-in-full saves $250).
- **Capacity:** 6 founder spots. Cohort starts **September 1, 2026**; enrollment closes **August 28, 2026** or when spots fill.
- **After this founder cohort:** standard price moves to $6,000.
- *(Previously $2,000 / 5×$500 with Aug 12/Aug 9 dates — changed after the first attempt at this cohort window produced zero signups; see `L2_OPERATING_DECISION_LOG.md`.)*

### Bonuses

1. **Founder Fast-Track Roadblock Audit** — worth $750 — private 60-min 1:1 before the cohort starts, first 3 founders only.
2. **Promotion Impact Audit Tool** — worth $500 — all cohort members; ranks 3 real projects on scope, leverage, business impact, visibility, leadership signal, and gaps.
3. **Performance Review Game Plan** — worth $750 — all cohort members; guided template completed near a review/checkpoint, reviewed by Mansour.
4. **Mock Behavioral Interview** — worth $750 — full 1:1 at program end, first 3 founders only.

> **Open question (unresolved):** whether the Promotion Impact Audit Tool bonus and the live IBB resume-bullet audit tool are the same deliverable or two separate builds.

### Guarantee

"Give the program 60 days. If you show up, complete Missions 1–6, ask for help when you're stuck, and still don't have a clear Promotion-Ready Plan plus a stronger way to make your impact visible, I'll buy it back from you in full. No hard feelings."

### Enrollment Mechanic — **updated Aug 2026**

The offer page's "Here's what to do next" is now:

1. DM me on Instagram and tell me you're in, out, or need more information.
2. Once you're in, I'll send you payment details.
3. Once payment is confirmed, you're in!

**There is no application form.** `offer/apply.html` is retired and orphaned (no live links point to it). `offer/vsl.html` was updated to point to the same Instagram DM CTA. This replaced a fully application-based, no-call enrollment model (apply → review → accepted → pay + Skool setup) that produced zero signups on its first cohort attempt.

## 5. Objection Handling & FAQ

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "What if it doesn't work?" | The 60-day buyback guarantee. |
| "Can't afford it right now" | A stalled promotion can mean $10K–$30K/year in missed income; $1,000 founder pricing (or 5×$250) is a fraction of that. |
| "Don't have enough time" | Weekly calls, 12 weeks to build the plan, 12 more to apply it inside your actual job — not a second job on top of your job. |
| "How much does it cost?" | $1,000 paid in full, or 5 payments of $250 ($1,250 total). Moves to $6,000 after this first cohort. |
| "Why 6 months?" | 12 weeks builds the plan; real promotion proof needs at least one work cycle to actually create. |
| "Is this 1-on-1 or group?" | Small founder cohort (6 spots), weekly group calls, Skool community from day one. |
| "Will this guarantee I get promoted?" | No — nobody honest can promise that. What's guaranteed is the proof, visibility, and trust-building process. |
| "What if I've only been at my company a year or two?" | Not for brand-new engineers still surviving their first year or two, and not for people getting weak reviews who need to fix the basics first. |
| "How do I actually join?" | DM on Instagram (in/out/need more info) → payment details once you're in → enrolled once payment is confirmed. No application, no call. |
