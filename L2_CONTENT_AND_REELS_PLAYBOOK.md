## Who We're Talking To

**One avatar. All content is written for this person. Never use "Invisible Performer" itself in client-facing copy — it's internal shorthand.**

| | The Invisible Performer |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | 2–5 years into engineering. Technically solid, good reviews, trusted to deliver — not yet seen as a promotion-ready leader. |
| **Core Pain** | Manager keeps saying "you're doing great," but the promotion isn't happening. Good work isn't getting credited past their own team. |
| **Emotional State** | Quietly wondering if being good at the job just isn't enough. Doesn't want to become "the annoying self-promoter." |
| **What They Want** | To be seen by their manager and senior people as already operating at the next level — a clear promotion case, better visibility, stronger relationships. |
| **Their Reality** | Closing tickets, helping the team, doing solid work — but the projects that would prove they're ready keep going to someone else. Vague "show more leadership" feedback nobody translates into English. |

## Core Content Principle: Share Value and Create an Invitation

Default to **we / us / our** for shared career experiences; use **I** for firsthand founder stories and promises; use **you** for direct CTAs, individualized examples, and eligibility. Share patterns and lessons learned rather than lecturing. Let the offer appear as a relevant next step, not proof the audience needs rescuing.

## The 5-Part Content Framework (Strategy Layer)

1. **Draw a Line in the Sand** — every post needs a shared-default vs. better-alternative contrast, with evidence and an invitation.
2. **Stretch the Gap** — use Results/Reality/Roadblocks levers plus Models, Metrics, Metaphors, and Questions to create recognition without shaming.
3. **Product Placement** — embed the offer mid-content as context, never as an end-of-post pitch. Comment **OFFER** ties to a client result; `launchtolead.io` ties to outcome-heavy, program-scope content. (Comment **IMPACT** → Impact Bullet Builder/lead magnet is not live yet — no destination exists until a new lead magnet is built.)
4. **The Easy Next Yes** — one CTA or CTE per post, matched to audience temperature (cold → free value; warm → Comment OFFER; hot → `launchtolead.io` and now specifically the Instagram DM ask on the offer page).
5. **Content Planning** — five questions before writing: what's shared, how do they self-recognize, what grounds the claim, what can they consider, where does the offer land naturally.

## The Content Recipe (Formatting Layer)

**Hook → Stack → Payoff → Invite.** Stack format is Story (preferred), List, or Steps. Payoff is the single most important idea/pivot. Invite is one CTA/CTE only.

## Content Angles

Problem, Proof, Philosophy, Shared Practice, PSA — all five still require the mandatory old/new contrast.

## Voice Rules

- Share, don't lecture. Invite, don't command.
- Default to we/us/our for shared experience; I for firsthand stories/promises; you for CTAs and individualized feedback.
- Banned generic buzzwords: game-changer, unlock your potential, secret sauce, hack, crush it, level up, cheat sheet.
- "Invisible" is allowed publicly — it's core positioning language (Invisible Impact). "Invisible Performer" as the avatar *name* stays internal.
- Never promise guaranteed promotion or job placement.

## The Magic Model (Internal Content-Pillar Map — Never Client-Facing)

**Goal:** Promotion-Ready Engineer. Three obstacle → milestone pairs, each cleared by 3 missions:

1. **Low-Leverage Tasks → Next-Level Work** — Master Workload, Train Others, Expand Scope.
2. **Invisible Impact → Known for Impact** — Demonstrate Value (maps to Impact Bullet Builder), Spread Impact, Reach Leaders.
3. **Unproven Leadership → Trusted by Seniors** — Meet Commitments, Own Outcomes, Influence Others.

Use it as a combinatorial content generator — pull a single Red (pure agitation), a single Green (pure outcome/proof), a single Mission (tactical how-to), or an Obstacle→Milestone pair (before/after) depending on what the post needs.

Removes the self-promotion/sleaze objection this avatar is most sensitive to.

## Instagram Reel Execution (summary)

**Hook → Recognition → Reframe → Framework → Proof → Payoff → One Invitation.** Hook must land in 2–3 seconds and communicate who it's for + what problem/idea it addresses. Recognition uses inclusive "a lot of us" language, never accusatory. One invitation only per Reel — never stack comment/save/share/follow/link asks.

Evidence standard: label every claim as platform-confirmed signal, supported behavioral principle, common creator practice, or experimental hypothesis — never present a hypothesis (exact cut interval, ideal length, posting frequency) as an algorithm fact.
