## Source Priority

1. The active canonical sections in this file control current decisions and instructions.
2. Archived context may be used for examples, reasoning, historical insight, rejected alternatives, and idea generation.
3. When archived or embedded source material conflicts with an active rule, the active canonical rule wins.
4. Do not revive a superseded avatar, offer, CTA, price, date, funnel, or strategy unless Mansour explicitly requests it.
5. Preserve useful language and reasoning from older material without treating old operational details as current.

## Proof Usage Rules

- Distinguish direct quotations from Mansour's summaries and inferences.
- Do not invent dates, compensation, titles, causal claims, or outcomes.
- Respect anonymity and permission restrictions recorded in the source.
- Current-avatar proof should be preferred for Promotion-Ready Engineer marketing.
- Legacy job-search stories may still be useful, but must be framed accurately and should not imply they came from the current six-month program.
- Named-client content requires the applicable results disclaimer: "Individual experiences presented here may not be typical. Your results may vary."
- Contact information in the raw source is retained for continuity only; do not reproduce it in public-facing content unless operationally necessary and explicitly requested.
