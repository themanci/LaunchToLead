Why Listen to Me

I know this path well because I’ve walked it myself. At John Deere, I went from entry-level engineer to junior engineer, scrum master, product owner, engineering manager, and then into a high-level AI and efficiency role across an entire division. All in 7 years.

I learned very quickly that good work matters and is the pre-requisite for promotion…but good work without visibility to the right audience doesn’t move your career very far.

I’ve also helped engineers make real moves: Austin V. went from stuck senior engineer to staff engineer after working together. Jonah B. and AJ M. moved from entry-level engineers to junior engineers and Karen B. went from a junior engineer at John Deere to a senior engineer at Google.

None of those moves happened because they suddenly became better engineers overnight. They were already good. The shift was that their work started creating clearer proof, the right people started seeing it, and their managers had a stronger case to bring into the room.

Different engineers. Different companies. Same pattern: good work starts moving further when the proof, visibility, and trust are there.

How It Works

We build your promotion case in three layers: better proof, better visibility, and more senior trust. We deliver those three layers across two 12-week sprints.

Layer 1

Next-Level Work

Get out of low-leverage tasks and into work that proves you’re ready for more — mastering your workload, making it teachable, and expanding your scope.

Layer 2

Known for Impact

Stop letting useful work disappear into Jira tickets, Slack threads, and one manager’s memory. Learn to demonstrate value and reach the leaders who need to see it.

Layer 3

Trusted by Seniors

Turn “solid engineer” into “person we can trust with more” — meeting commitments, owning outcomes, and influencing without authority.

The first 12 weeks are the Build Sprint: kickoff, 9 core missions, and a closeout session that turns those three layers into your Promotion-Ready Plan. The next 12 weeks are the Application Sprint, where we take what’s happening inside your job each week and turn it into the next move — the manager conversation, the project to chase, the update to send, or the leadership signal to create.

By the end of the first 60 days, you should have a clear Promotion-Ready Plan, a better read on what proof you’re missing, and a stronger way to make your impact visible without feeling like a self-promotion gremlin.
