## Repo Overrides (decisions made in this repo after the ChatGPT project's July 27–28 build, which supersede it)

- **Funnel mechanic:** As of **Aug 20, 2026**, the offer page CTA is application-based again via a short, revived `offer/apply.html` (8 fields: name, email, years of experience, current title/level, desired role + timeline, what's making you feel stuck, time zone, submit) → reviewed within 48 hours → next steps. Formspree endpoint `xvzjkwnb` confirmed live via a test submission on 2026-08-20. This reverses the brief **Aug 12–20, 2026** DM-only mechanic ("DM me on Instagram and tell me you're in, out, or need more information" → payment details → enrolled), which itself had replaced the original, more elaborate application form. `offer/vsl.html` still points to the Instagram DM CTA and was not touched in the Aug 20 rewrite.
- **Cohort dates:** Cohort now starts **September 15, 2026**; sign-up closes **September 11, 2026** or when spots fill. (Was Sept 1 / Aug 28 from Aug 12–20, 2026; Aug 12 / Aug 9 in the original ChatGPT files.)
- **Capacity:** **10 spots** (was 6 "founder" spots from Aug 12–20, 2026).
- **Pricing:** **$1,000 paid in full, or 5 payments of $250** ($1,250 total — paid-in-full saves $250) — unchanged since the prior override. **The $6,000 standard/future price anchor has been removed** (Aug 20, 2026) — it read as a marketing gimmick to the engineer avatar. Do not reintroduce it without Mansour's explicit request.
- **Bonuses removed:** The founder-only bonuses (Founder Fast-Track Roadblock Audit, Mock Behavioral Interview) and the named tools with attached dollar values (Promotion Impact Audit Tool — $500, Performance Review Game Plan — $750) were all cut from the offer page (Aug 20, 2026). Feedback was that dollar-valued bonus stacking reads as hypey/cringy marketing to this engineer avatar.
- **Offer doc structure and voice — full rewrite (Aug 20, 2026):** `offer/index.html` was rewritten wholesale in a plain, no-hype, spec-sheet style: no italic hook quotes, no highlighter-style `<mark>` emphasis on sentences, no rhetorical asides or jokes, no repeated restatement of the same point across sections. Doc title changed to "Build a stronger case for your next engineering promotion." Structure now: intro (offer + 3 focus areas) → Who This Is For → What We Work On (3 subsections) → How The Program Works (first 3 months / next 3 months) → Why This Is A Group Program → What You Should Have By The End → The Guarantee → Cost → Is This A Good Fit? (yes/no grid) → Proof This Works (links to Instagram and `success-stories.html` instead of narrating client wins on-page) → 3-question FAQ (trimmed from 6; kept only questions that add new information, cut ones that just restated body copy) → Interested? (apply CTA). This supersedes the Aug 2026 restructure described in the line below, which is now historical.
- *(Historical — superseded by the row above) Offer doc structure:* `offer/index.html` was restructured (Aug 2026) per outside copywriting feedback: "Is This You?" appeared before the three-layer model, deliverables used "so you…" benefit language, and the page used visual section eyebrows/cards instead of a flat wall of text.
- **Reason for override:** the first cohort using the original application-based funnel produced zero signups, which led to the DM-only, lower-friction pivot. ## Hansen Beck Supplemental Source Decision

### Status

`L2_HANSEN_BECK_SUPPLEMENTAL_SOURCE_LIBRARY.md` is active as a **supplemental source**, not a canonical replacement framework. It preserves the supplied Hansen Beck PDF text layers, summarizes their relevance to Launch to Lead, and contains a matched interpretation of the supplied concept-card fronts and backs.

### Permitted Uses

- Deepen the nine mission buildouts with supporting leadership, communication, influence, personal-effectiveness, project-management, and application principles.
- Generate coaching questions, practice exercises, reflection prompts, workplace assignments, and completion evidence.
- Provide optional raw material for engineering-specific social content.
- Strengthen Launch to Lead's own sales communication where relevant.

### Routing

- **Program or mission design:** Begin with the Master Brand and Offer Context and current Magic Model; consult the Hansen Beck library for supporting mechanisms.
- **Social content:** Begin with the Content and Reels Playbook; consult the Hansen Beck library when it materially strengthens the chosen mission, problem, story, or behavior.
- **Conflicts:** Active Launch to Lead decisions always win.
- **Normal prompts:** The supplemental file may be used automatically. Mansour does not need to request Hansen Beck explicitly each time.

### Adaptation and Evidence Rules

- Translate concepts into original Launch to Lead language and engineering workplace examples.
- Do not reproduce Hansen Beck card designs, branded tools, diagrams, exercises, stories, or substantial proprietary wording without permission.
- Independently verify numerical, scientific, historical, quotation, and research claims before using them publicly as evidence.
- Do not allow an interesting general leadership concept to displace the content north star: attracting the Invisible Performer toward the Promotion-Ready Engineer Blueprint.
